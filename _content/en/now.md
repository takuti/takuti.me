<i class="fa fa-map-marker"></i> Summer in Ottawa is so pleasant. Moderately energetic, several local events, not too busy, not too hot, with a usual sense of community.

### 4 days catching up with friends in the states

I visited my friends in Austin, TX, last week. First international trip in 10 months! There had been a little bit of psychological struggle over the past few months, so it was great to break the routine and detach myself from the internal noise.

### 8 months of practicing French

While building my interdisciplinary and international career from Canada's capital, I remain committed to promoting English-French bilingualism in my work. Thanks to regularly practicing French since January, my capacity is slowly yet steadily improving at the intermediate level. I recently conducted a [30-min mentoring session](https://adplist.org/mentors/takuya-kitazawa) in French, which was especially rewarding and encouraging for me.

### 4 years as self-employed

As the 4th anniversary of [being independent](/note/becoming-a-freelancer-in-canada/) approaches, I decided to take a new opportunity to promote [humane technology](/note/foundations-of-humane-technology/) in a more impactful and scalable way than ever. Nothing fundamentally changes in terms of how I think, [write](/note/), and act, but in the AI-dominated world where tools - not people - easily become the primary subject of our work, I feel I still have a lot more to do in the field.
